About:

My name is Maximiliano, my main goal in life is to become a big comic book artist, or someone known for their skill. Here I have compiled a few of my works that I have made this year. All are pretty recent but I am confident I will become much better in the future and over time. I hope that my aspiration and ambition of becoming known for my skill will cause me to venture into territories that I never thought possible. >

This website will serve as a bookmark to where my skill level was at during fall of 2026. I intend to improve every component of each skill I know. I don't only want to master and learn more about drawing comic books, but I want to learn and experience as much as I can. Open to new possibilities.
