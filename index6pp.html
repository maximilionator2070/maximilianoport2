About Me

The Artist.

My name is Maximiliano. My main goal in life is to become a major comic book artist, or someone known for their skill. Here I have compiled a few of my works that I made this year. They are all recent, but I am confident that I will become much better over time.

I hope my aspiration and ambition will lead me into territories I never thought possible. This website serves as a snapshot of my skill level during fall 2026.

I intend to improve every component of each skill I know. I do not only want to master comic book drawing, but also learn and experience as much as I can while remaining open to new possibilities.
